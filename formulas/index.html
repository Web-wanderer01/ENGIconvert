Chemistry

Molarity

M = n / V

Moles of solute per liter of solution.

Chemistry

pH Equation

pH = -log[H+]

Measure of hydrogen ion concentration.

Physics

Newton's 2nd Law

F = ma

Force equals mass times acceleration.

Finance

Compound Interest

A = P(1+r/n)^(nt)

Interest calculated on the initial principal.

Mechanics

Newton’s Second Law

F = m × a

Force equals mass multiplied by acceleration.

Energy

Kinetic Energy

KE = ½mv²

Energy of an object due to its motion.

Electrical

Ohm’s Law

V = I × R

Voltage equals current times resistance.

Waves

Wave Velocity

v = f × λ

Frequency multiplied by wavelength.

Mathematics

Quadratic Formula

x = (−b ± √D) / 2a

Where D = b² − 4ac.

Thermodynamics

Ideal Gas Law

PV = nRT

Relates pressure, volume, amount, and temperature.

Kinematics

Acceleration

a = (v − u) / t

Change in velocity over time.

Kinematics

Motion Equation

v = u + at

Final velocity for uniformly accelerated motion.

Momentum

Linear Momentum

p = mv

Mass multiplied by velocity.

Fluids

Fluid Pressure

P = ρgh

Pressure caused by a fluid column.

Thermal

Heat Transfer

Q = mcΔT

Heat needed to change a substance’s temperature.

Electrical Power

Rₑq = R₁ + R₂ + R₃

Equivalent resistance in a parallel circuit.

Circle Area

Area of a circle with radius r.

Fundamental identity for right-triangle functions.
